Chitkara University Courses Overview

Chitkara University (Punjab & Himachal campuses) provides a wide range of Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), and PhD programs across various disciplines. The university is NAAC A+ accredited and UGC recognized.

Major Undergraduate Programs

Engineering & Technology (BE/B.Tech)

  • Specialisations: CSE, AI & ML, Software Engineering, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, ECE, Automobile, Mechatronics, etc.
  • Fees: ₹6 lakh - ₹25.9 lakh
  • Eligibility: 10+2 with 60% in PCM + JEE Main

Architecture & Design

  • B.Arch - ₹10.10 lakh (12th with PCM + NATA)
  • B.Des - Animation, Fashion, Interior, Product Design, UX Design, etc. (₹12.10 lakh)

Management & Commerce

  • BBA (General, Digital Sciences, FinTech & AI, Aviation, Retail, Sports, etc.)
  • Fees: ₹3.55 lakh - ₹12.10 lakh
  • B.Com / B.Com with ACCA

IT & Computer Applications

  • BCA
  • BCA with AI & ML
  • Fees: ₹3.5 lakh - ₹5.6 lakh

Pharmacy & Health Sciences

  • B.Pharm - ₹8.10 lakh
  • B.Sc Nursing
  • B.Sc Allied Health Sciences

Arts, Humanities & Media

  • BA Psychology
  • BA Journalism & Mass Communication
  • BA Public Policy
