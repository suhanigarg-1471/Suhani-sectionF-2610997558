About Us

General Information

Chitkara University, established in 2010 in Punjab and 2009 in Himachal Pradesh, is managed by the Chitkara Educational Trust and has campuses near Chandigarh (Punjab) and Solan (Himachal Pradesh). It is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC. The university spans a 70-acre campus in Punjab and offers modern infrastructure, including ICT-equipped classrooms, laboratories, and student facilities.

Accreditations and Affiliations

Chitkara University is approved and affiliated with multiple regulatory bodies:

  • AICTE, PCI, NCTE, INC, BCI, COA, NCHMCT
  • Member of AIU, AUAP, IET UK, and UNAI

Research, Innovation, and Global Exposure

Chitkara University promotes research and innovation with over ₹107 crore in research funding, centers of excellence, and a startup incubation ecosystem.

Students benefit from global pathway programs, enabling them to earn degrees from partner universities in the United States, Canada, and Australia.
