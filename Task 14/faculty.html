Faculty

Chitkara University faculty comprises experienced educators, industry practitioners, and researchers across diverse disciplines, committed to academic excellence and student development.

Overview of Faculty

Chitkara University’s faculty represents a blend of scholars, industry experts, and researchers who bring decades of combined experience in their respective fields. They are actively involved in teaching, mentoring, and research, ensuring students gain both theoretical knowledge and practical skills. Faculty members maintain strong industry connections, facilitating internships, live projects, and networking opportunities for students, while emphasizing critical thinking, ethical decision-making, and innovation in their teaching approach.

Business School Faculty

At Chitkara Business School, faculty members specialize in areas such as Strategic Management, Strategic Marketing, Brand Design Strategies, Marketing Management, and Business Research. They hold credentials from premier global institutions and have extensive corporate experience, bridging the gap between theory and real-world business applications. Faculty members also serve as academic advisors and career mentors, guiding students in entrepreneurship, research, and professional development.

Engineering and Technology Faculty

The engineering and technology faculty includes experts in Information Theory, Data Mining, Waste Management, Highway Engineering, Road Pavements, Traffic Engineering, DBMS, Data Analytics, and Machine Learning applications. They focus on both academic research and practical applications, including advanced software tools and precision agriculture technologies. Faculty members are engaged in cutting-edge research and provide guidance on projects that integrate engineering principles with real-world challenges.

Pharmacy Faculty

The Pharmacy faculty at Chitkara University includes professionals like Dr. Sandeep Arora, who has over 20 years of experience spanning pharmaceutical production, quality assurance, teaching, and research management. Faculty members in this school focus on both academic instruction and research in pharmaceutical sciences, ensuring students are well-prepared for careers in healthcare and research.

Faculty Engagement and Mentorship

Across all departments, Chitkara University faculty are committed to student success beyond the classroom. They act as research collaborators, career mentors, and advisors, investing personally in students’ professional journeys. Their teaching emphasizes interactive learning, case discussions, and practical exposure, fostering an environment that encourages innovation and entrepreneurial thinking.

Accessing Faculty Information

Detailed profiles of faculty members, including their qualifications, areas of expertise, and academic achievements, are available on the university’s official websites for each school, such as the Business School, Pharmacy, and Engineering departments. These profiles provide insights into individual faculty members’ research interests, professional experience, and contributions to academia and industry.
